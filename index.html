How to install
1
Download the client
Get the APK from the X7 download link. On Android allow install from unknown sources when prompted.
2
Let resources unpack
First launch downloads the asset packs — use Wi-Fi and keep the app open.
3
Pick a server
New servers reset rankings — check the Server tab for the next opening time.
4
Bind your account
Sign in with Facebook or your Jodo account so progress survives a reinstall.

Day one tips

Save diamonds for the first legendary rotation. Claim all three daily Vit Gift windows — friendship vit is the slowest resource in the game. Weather & events follow Hong Kong server time (HKT) — pick your country in Themes & time so times show in your local clock.

300 Advanced
Myst Tickets

Via Unishard Exchange · Happy Exchange

Steps
1
Save 300 Unishards
Each exchange costs 50 Unishards for 10 Overlord Stickers, and you need 60 stickers — so 6 exchanges × 50 Unishards = 300 total. Stack those up ahead of time.
2
Get 60 Overlord Stickers
In the Happy Exchange, match the exchange below exactly — 50 Unishards for 10 stickers — and do it 6 times. Don't buy any other item in that exchange list.
3
Recycle the stickers
When the Recycle event comes around, recycle those 60 stickers for Recycle Points.
4
Buy 300 Advanced Myst Tickets
Spend the Recycle Points to buy 300 Advanced Myst Tickets straight from the Recycle shop — confirm it's the "Advanced" one, like the ticket pictured above.
